StimPad: Stimming Soundboard

95 stim sounds, one tap.

StimPad is a sensory soundboard with 95 stim sounds on one screen: vacuum hum, hair dryer, sirens, pen clicks, bubble wrap, keyboard thock, rain, fans, and white, pink or brown noise. Tap once to play, and loop a sound for as long as you like.

Big buttons, a clear layout, and playback that behaves the same way every time. No account, no sign-up. Every sound is built into the app.

Free

  • 14 sounds to keep, including rain, ocean waves, white noise, desk fan, vacuum hum, pen click, camera shutter, campfire, clock tick, and sirens
  • An 8-second preview of any locked Plus sound
  • Watch one ad to open every sound for 20 minutes, once a day
  • Favorites saved on your device

On iPhone

  • One Plus sound is free each day, rotating through all 81 Plus sounds
  • Recents: recent sounds are one tap away, and StimPad remembers your last sound and settings
  • An optional daily reminder, off unless you turn it on
  • Requires iOS 17 or later on iPhone and iPad

StimPad Plus

A one-time purchase. No subscription. It unlocks all 95 sounds and removes ads. Restore purchases on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, or the same Google account on Android.

Features

  • Tap to play or stop; short sounds replay until you press Stop
  • Loop any sound until you stop it
  • Sleep timer: 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or until you stop
  • Head Flossing: the sound sweeps slowly from ear to ear (best with headphones)
  • Pitch and speed control
  • Haptic taps and optional tap ripples
  • 12 categories: Clicks, Household, Retro, Water, Nature, Noise, Alarms, Bells, Vehicles, Animals, Tools and Misc
  • Works offline after install
  • English, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese

Anyone who enjoys sensory sounds, including many neurodivergent people who use sound to stim, focus or unwind.

StimPad is reviewed and listed by BridgingApps, a program of Easterseals. It is a sensory sound toy, not a medical or therapy tool.
